    Frequently asked questions

    How the work is organised and what it costs.

    What is payment cascading and why does it matter?

    Cascading re-sends a declined transaction through another provider according to set rules. It recovers deposits that would otherwise be lost to technical failures, issuer declines or the limits of a single PSP. It should be tuned by decline code: not every decline is worth retrying.

    How do you increase deposit approval rates?

    We break approval rates down by provider, method, geo, BIN and issuing bank, find the weak segments and change routing and cascade rules, the cashier’s method set and its UX. Changes are rolled out step by step and measured against the baseline.

    How much does a payments audit cost?

    The intro call is free. An expert consultation is €100–150 per hour. An initial audit of the website, gateway and analytics for Tier 1 starts at €600. A full optimisation cycle with implementation support typically costs €3,000–4,000, and monthly support starts at €1,000 per month. The final price depends on how much product access you can give and how many regions we review.

    Which geos do you work with?

    Our core expertise is Tier 1: EU, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. We also handle difficult markets such as the Netherlands, the UK and France. Tier 2–3 on request.

    Do you sell PSPs or payment orchestrators?

    No. We look at payments from the operator’s side and compare options for your task. The choice of providers and tools stays with you.

    What access do you need for an audit, and do you sign an NDA?

    We agree on the scope of access on the intro call: from viewing the cashier and analytics to gateway settings. We sign an NDA before any data is shared if needed.
