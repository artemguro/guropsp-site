When you need an audit

Typical situations operators come to us with.

01

Approval rate dropped, cause unclear

The provider says “everything works on our side”, yet deposits are down on last week.

02

Lots of declines, no response

“Do not honor” and “Insufficient funds” pile up in the logs, but nobody breaks them down by bank and BIN.

03

One PSP carries almost all volume

Any outage at that provider and the cashier in a key geo stops.

04

Many sign-ups, few deposits

Players reach the cashier and leave: wrong method, extra fields, an unclear error.

05

Fees keep growing

Provider terms haven’t been revisited since launch, while your volume has changed a lot.

06

No picture in numbers

You hear about problems from support or players, not from a dashboard.

What we check

Six areas where deposits get lost.

01

Player path to the cashier

Registration, verification, getting to the cashier. We look for extra steps, fields and points where players drop the deposit.

02

Cashier

Methods by geo and their order, default amounts, currencies, error messages, mobile version, repeat deposits.

03

Gateway

3DS settings, timeouts, card storage and tokenisation, status and callback handling.

04

Routing and cascading

Rules by geo, BIN and issuing bank, which decline codes are cascaded, compliance with Visa and Mastercard retry limits.

05

Providers

Approval rate and stability of each PSP, pricing, fees, reserves, settlement times, contract terms.

06

Analytics and processes

Which metrics are tracked, who monitors approval rates, how and when an issue is escalated to a provider.

Frequently asked questions

How is an audit different from a one-off consultation?

A consultation answers a specific question. In an audit we go through the entire deposit path ourselves, review settings and data, and come back with a report, a loss estimate and a plan of changes.

Do we need to pause payment acceptance?

No. The audit runs on your live setup: we review settings and statistics and go through the cashier as a player. Changes are then rolled out step by step without pausing payments.

Does an audit make sense for a new project with no payment history?

Yes. Instead of analysing statistics we review the chosen gateway, providers, methods by geo and routing scheme before launch, so you don’t lose deposits from day one.

What access do you need?

We agree on the scope of access on the intro call: from viewing the cashier and analytics to gateway settings. We sign an NDA before any data is shared if needed.

How much does a payments audit cost?

An initial audit of the website, gateway and analytics for Tier 1 starts at €600. A full optimisation cycle with implementation support typically costs €3,000–4,000.
