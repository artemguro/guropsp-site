Reputation

GURO ID

In iGaming, a CV says what a person claims. GURO ID shows what was confirmed. You name the counterparty, they confirm in Telegram, and only then the record appears in both profiles.

✓

Confirmed by both sides

Without the second side the record stays a draft, so a rating cannot be inflated alone.

✓

Private by default

Counterparty names and amounts are hidden. Public is the fact that two sides confirmed a partnership.

✓

Rating that stays yours

It belongs to the person, not the company, and survives a change of job.
